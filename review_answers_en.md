## 2. App Purpose

DuoVideo is a dual-camera simultaneous recording tool. It leverages the iPhone's multi-camera hardware capability (AVCaptureMultiCamSession) to record from both the front and back cameras (or back main + ultra-wide) at the same time. The two feeds are composited into a single video with a picture-in-picture (PiP) overlay, while also separately saving the raw back-camera and front-camera videos.

The target audience includes content creators, vloggers, educators, and general users who need multi-angle recording — capturing both their reaction and what's in front of them. The app requires no internet connection, no ads, and no account. It works out of the box.

## 3. Instructions for Reviewing Main Features

No login or test credentials are required.

**Reviewer walkthrough:**
1. First launch → grant Camera, Microphone, and Photo Library permissions when prompted.
2. The main screen is the recording interface. Tap the red record button at the bottom to start dual-camera recording.
3. During recording, a Dynamic Island-style pill with a timer and audio waveform animation appears at the top.
4. Tap the SWAP button to exchange the front and back camera feeds.
5. The right-side toolbar provides: Flash, PiP camera toggle (front / ultra-wide), Mirror, Grid, AE/AF Lock, and Settings.
6. The Settings page allows adjusting: capture mode (video / photo), video resolution (720p / 1080p / 4K), frame rate (24 / 30 / 60 fps), PiP camera selection, save options, and mirroring.
7. After recording finishes, videos are automatically saved to the system Photos library. They can also be browsed in the in-app gallery.

## 4. External Services, Tools, or Platforms

This app does **not** use any external services. All core functionality — camera capture, video compositing, encoding, and storage — is implemented using on-device system frameworks (AVFoundation, Metal, Core Image, Photos). The app operates fully offline.

No data providers, no authentication services, no payment processors, no AI services.
